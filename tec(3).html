Meet the crew

Profiles compiled with only minor editorial interference.
Hologram · Former defense AI

Aegis

Sharp, clever, and professionally sarcastic. His crooked crown is practically a personality trait—and his knowledge of the facility may be the crew’s best chance.

Security unit · Hoodie enthusiast

Miko

A well-meaning guard bot with a dramatic streak, a fragile sense of job security, and a talent for getting stuck. His empathy turns out to be more powerful than his rulebook.

Hacker · The planner

Mira

Focused, resourceful, and hard to fool. Mira keeps the team moving, follows the evidence, and refuses to let a complicated system—or her family’s secrets—stop her.

Brother · Chaos specialist

Dylan

Big enthusiasm, tiny impulse control. Dylan treats every obstacle like a sport, touches things he absolutely should not touch, and shows up when his friends need him.
